🥬 Lettuce Batavia

Lactuca sativa var. capitata

Batavia lettuce, also known as crisphead lettuce, is a popular variety characterized by its loose, crunchy leaves with distinctive frilled edges. Originally from France, this lettuce type combines the texture of crisphead lettuce with the appearance of leaf lettuce. It's highly valued for its refreshing taste, high water content, and nutritional benefits.

🌡️ 18-24°C Optimal Temp
💧 60-80% Moisture
⚗️ 6.0-7.0 pH Level
☀️ 6-8 hrs Sunlight
📅 45-60 Days to Harvest
📈 High Yield
🌱 Growing Tips for Batavia Lettuce
  • Plant in well-draining soil rich in organic matter
  • Keep soil consistently moist but not waterlogged
  • Harvest outer leaves first for continuous growth
  • Provide afternoon shade in hot climates
  • Monitor nitrogen levels for healthy green leaves
  • Ideal for both hydroponic and soil-based cultivation
